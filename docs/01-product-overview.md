# Product Overview and Personas

**Concept draft · 2026-11-18**

## Promise

**AI Music Radio — Own Your Music. Discover Its Stories.**

A local-first personal music server and AI radio producer. Users choose one identified track; AI researches its background, curates related recordings, explains musical connections and creates an enjoyable narrated episode. This is not a random playlist with AI announcements.

## The problem

Cloud subscriptions carry recurring costs and changing catalogs. Collections span personal files and services. Traditional music servers offer control but limited interpretation; algorithmic playlists offer discovery but rarely explain it.

A local library of legitimately acquired files remains playable independently of streaming subscriptions. Hardware, power, maintenance and optional AI services still have costs.

## Persona 1: Music Owner

**Who:** Collector of purchased digital music, CD rips and FLAC/ALAC/MP3, perhaps stored on NAS or desktop.

**Need:** A stable, personally controlled music library without continued streaming subscriptions.

**Scenario:** Rediscover a Radiohead track in the home server; let AI create a radio show connecting it to other recordings already owned.

**Value:** Music ownership + intelligent rediscovery.

## Persona 2: Music Explorer

**Who:** An enthusiast interested in indie rock, jazz, electronic, classical and music beyond repetitive recommendations.

**Need:** Recommendations with interesting, defensible explanations.

**Scenario:** From Radiohead’s layered arpeggios, explore the minimal space of The xx and electronic textures of Tycho.

**Value:** Discovery with context. These are manually suggested candidate links, not validated model output.

## Persona 3: Deep Listener

**Who:** Listener interested in composition, history, arrangement, recording versions and cultural context.

**Need:** Music knowledge woven into playback rather than fragmented searches.

**Scenario:** Choose one orchestral recording track. AI introduces its context in the larger composition and related recordings, without needing to locate passages within the file.

**Value:** Listening with understanding.

## Product journey

Choose identified track → Validate metadata → Research with provenance → Plan editorial theme and musical transitions → Produce TTS and original/licensed BGM → Orchestrate authorized playback → Save episode or explore another recording.

## Positioning

**Music server = enduring foundation. AI Radio = differentiated experience.**

The library remains useful when AI generation is off. For the first demo, focus on music with reliable, meaningful interpretation material, not all audio types. Unknown recording identity can return a clear error. Optional cloud streaming is subject to actual provider permissions.

Primary entry audiences: **Music Owner + Music Explorer**. Deep Listener demonstrates interpretive depth.
