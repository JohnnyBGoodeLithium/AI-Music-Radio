# Monochrome Low-Fi Wireframes

English | [简体中文](06-lowfi-wireframes.zh-CN.md)

**Direction only, not an implemented UI or an official Lenovo/Qira design system.**

White/gray canvas, dark type, Notion-like content hierarchy. Use **mostly straight separators, simple blocks and sparse dashed borders** for draft AI suggestions. Sketch feeling means a legible working page, **not** the Sketch app, fake handwriting, or an Axure form full of controls. Defer Qira brand styling/tokens.

```text
YOUR MUSIC                             Library  Discover  Radio
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Your next music story                  [Ask AI]

┌────────────────────────┐ ┌────────────────────────┐
│ CONTINUE A THREAD      │ │ RECENT INTERESTS       │
│ [Listen / revise]      │ │ [Listen / revise]      │
└────────────────────────┘ └────────────────────────┘

┌ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┐
  AI EDITOR'S NOTE: another music trail
  [Why?]                  [Change topic]
└ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┄ ┘

PERSONAL MUSIC REPORT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Collection trends      Artist network       Evidence / sources
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
NOW PLAYING                              [Prev] [Play] [Next]
```

Keep music playable while browsing reports. Distinguish evidenced insights from editable AI proposals. Validate mobile/narrow layouts after the core experience works.
